# Prosthetic Devices

## Trans-radial Prosthesis Overview

The Trans-radial prosthesis is a device designed to replace the function of the hand after a trans-radial amputation. It can be operated mechanically or electrically. It consists of a socket and a terminal device that emulates the appearance and grasping function of the human hand. Existing trans-radial prosthetic devices can be classified into two parts. They are **commercial prosthetics** and **research level prosthetics**. **Commercial prosthetics** are designed to meet user needs, and **research level prosthetics** focus on innovative technologies. 

**Tendon-driven mechanisms** are used in many adaptive grasping prosthetic hands. Research-level designs frequently aim for under-actuation by reducing the number of actuators while increasing degrees of freedom. **DC motors** are commonly used in both commercial and research prosthetics for precise motion control.

Additionally, commercial prosthetic devices increasingly incorporate advanced features such as **smartphone-based user interfaces** to improve usability and controllability.

## Existing Commercial and Research-Level Prosthetic Devices

### iGrasp
- **Mechanism:** Tendon-based mechanisms  
- **Degrees of Freedom (DOF):** 1  
- **Actuators:** 6 DC motors  

### UomPro Hand
- **Mechanism:** Gear-based mechanisms  
- **Degrees of Freedom (DOF):** 1  
- **Actuators:** 6 DC motors  

### The Yale MyoAdapt Hand
- **Mechanism:** Tendon-based mechanisms, crossed bar mechanisms  
- **Degrees of Freedom (DOF):** 1  
- **Actuators:** 1 DC motor  

### Smart Hand
- **Mechanism:** Tendon-based mechanisms, torsional spring-based mechanisms  
- **Degrees of Freedom (DOF):** 3  
- **Actuators:** 4 DC motors  

### FLLEX Hand
- **Mechanism:** Tendon-based mechanisms  
- **Degrees of Freedom (DOF):** 3  
- **Actuators:** DC motors  

### Mobio
- **Mechanism:** Gear-based mechanisms, linkage-based mechanisms  
- **Degrees of Freedom (DOF):** 1  
- **Actuators:** DC motors  

### SoftHand Pro
- **Mechanism:** Gear-based mechanisms, linkage-based mechanisms  
- **Degrees of Freedom (DOF):** 1  
- **Actuators:** 1 DC motor  

### Prisma Hand II
- **Mechanism:** Tendon-based mechanisms  
- **Degrees of Freedom (DOF):** 1  
- **Actuators:** 3 DC motors
