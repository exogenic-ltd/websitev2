## Spatial Awareness for the Visually Impaired

**Vision Aid** empowers visually impaired individuals with a new sense of spatial perception. Using miniaturized LIDAR technology embedded in a stylish frame, the glasses scan the environment and translate obstacles into directional haptic feedback on the temples, creating a "tactile map" of the surroundings.

---

## How It Works

1.  **Scan:** LIDAR sensors Create a 3D point cloud of the immediate environment (up to 5m).
2.  **Process:** On-board processor identifies obstacles, overhangs, and drop-offs.
3.  **Feedback:** Vibration motors in the temple tips actuate to indicate direction and proximity of hazards.

---

## Features

- **Obstacle Detection:** Detects objects from knee to head height.
- **Navigation Assist:** Integration with GPS for turn-by-turn haptic guidance.
- **Face Recognition:** Auditory announcements of friends and family (via bone conduction).
- **All-Day Wear:** Lightweight design (< 50g) with 16-hour battery.
