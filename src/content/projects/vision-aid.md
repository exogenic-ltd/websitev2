## Concept Stage

This project is currently in the **concept generation and proposal stage**. Our team is actively exploring innovative approaches to spatial awareness technology for the visually impaired.

The vision is to create stylish glasses with embedded LIDAR technology that translate environmental obstacles into directional haptic feedback, creating a "tactile map" of the surroundings.
