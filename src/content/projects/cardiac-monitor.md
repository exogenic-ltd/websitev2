## Continuous Cardiac Telemetry

The **Cardiac Monitor** is a state-of-the-art wearable device designed to provide real-time ECG monitoring for patients with arrhythmias or those at high risk of cardiac events. Unlike traditional Holter monitors, this device is unobtrusive, water-resistant, and capable of transmitting data continuously to healthcare providers.

---

## Features

- **Real-Time Analysis:** On-device algorithms detect abnormalities instantly.
- **Cloud Integration:** Securely transmits data to the ExoGenic Health Cloud for doctor review.
- **Long Battery Life:** Up to 7 days of continuous monitoring on a single charge.
- **Patient Alerts:** Haptic feedback alerts the patient to take medication or seek help.

---

## Technical Specifications

- **Sensors:** 3-lead ECG, SpO2, Accelerometer.
- **Connectivity:** Bluetooth 5.0, LTE-M.
- **Weight:** 25g.
- **Water Resistance:** IP67.
