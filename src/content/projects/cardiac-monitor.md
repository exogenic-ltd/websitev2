## Concept Stage

This project is currently in the **concept generation and proposal stage**. Our team is actively exploring innovative approaches to continuous cardiac monitoring through wearable IoT technology.

The vision is to create a state-of-the-art device that provides real-time ECG monitoring for patients with arrhythmias or those at high risk of cardiac events.
