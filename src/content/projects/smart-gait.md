## Adaptive Lower-Limb Prosthetic

**Smart Gait** brings intelligent mobility to lower-limb amputees. By integrating advanced gyroscope and accelerometer data, the prosthetic ankle adjusts its stiffness and angle in real-time, simulating the natural biomechanics of a human foot on varying terrains—from stairs to rocky paths.

---

## Innovation

- **Active Damping:** Micro-hydraulic fluid active damping system controlled by AI.
- **Terrain Recognition:** Predicts the user's next step type (level, incline, decline) with 95% accuracy.
- **Energy Return:** Carbon fiber leaf spring stores and releases energy for a more efficient gait.
- **App Control:** Users can fine-tune sensitivity and modes via a smartphone app.

---

## Performance

- **Stiffness Range:** 5Nm/deg to Rigid.
- **Response Time:** < 10ms.
- **Battery Life:** 48 Hours.
