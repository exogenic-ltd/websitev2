## Concept Stage

This project is currently in the **concept generation and proposal stage**. Our team is actively exploring innovative approaches to intelligent mobility for lower-limb amputees.

The vision is to develop a prosthetic ankle that integrates advanced sensors and AI to adjust stiffness in real-time, simulating natural biomechanics across varying terrains.
