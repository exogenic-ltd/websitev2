## Neural Rehabilitation Interface

**Neuro-Link** bridges the gap between the brain and digital environments. Designed primarily for stroke rehabilitation, this non-invasive EEG headset allows patients to control gamified rehabilitation exercises using motor imagery, promoting neural plasticity and faster recovery.

---

## Key Capabilities

- **High-Fidelity EEG:** 16-channel dry-electrode system for precise signal acquisition.
- **Brain-Computer Interface (BCI):** Translates thought patterns into cursor or character movements.
- **Gamified Therapy:** Engaging VR/AR games that make repetitive rehabilitation exercises fun.
- **Progress Tracking:** Detailed analytics on neural engagement and motor recovery.

---

## Applications

- **Stroke Recovery:** Retraining neural pathways for limb control.
- **Assistive Technology:** Hands-free control for individuals with severe paralysis.
- **Cognitive Training:** enhancing focus and mental agility.
